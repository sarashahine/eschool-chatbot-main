LLM Answer:
Parents are Informed Guardians. The eSchool platform provides tools for parents to stay informed and engaged in their child's education. They can track their child's school bus in real-time with live GPS and receive arrival alerts for safety. The platform offers features for seamless communication with teachers through posts, chat, comments, and likes. Parents can also access comprehensive data on their child's academic performance, attendance, and behavior, and participate in polls and evaluations. They can switch between multiple children from a single dashboard.
